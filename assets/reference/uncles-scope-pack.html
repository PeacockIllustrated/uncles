05 Scope

What's in, what's out

Clear boundaries protect both of us. The monthly fee covers everything below in the "in" column. Anything in the "out" column is happily quoted separately as a Phase 2 piece of work.

Українською

Що входить у щомісячну плату, а що ні. Все, що поза цим списком, можна зробити окремо як додатковий проєкт.

✓ In Scope · £20/m covers
  • Hosting, server costs and software access
  • Up to 4 minor menu updates per month
  • Price changes and item description tweaks
  • Adding or removing one item per update
  • Emergency fixes if something breaks
  • Tier 3 dashboard remains free for you to use any time
✕ Out of Scope · Quoted separately
  • Major redesigns or restructures of the menu layout
  • New screens, new menu types, or new outlets
  • Custom integrations (POS, ordering systems, payments)
  • Marketing campaigns or social media content
  • Photography, food styling, or video
  • Print design beyond the menu PDF (cards, posters, packaging)

07 Terms

The honest bit

Friend pricing depends on this being clear and fair. Two simple rules to protect both of us.

Українською

Прості правила, щоб усе було чесно і без непорозумінь.

01 · Payment
Two simple payments

Hardware cost (Android sticks) is paid upfront so I can order and configure them ready for delivery. The remaining balance is paid on launch day, once everything is installed and you're trading. Monthly fee starts from the day the screens go live and runs for a minimum of 6 months. Cancel any time after that with 30 days notice.

02 · What you keep
Yours to keep

Brand pack, menu PDF, screen designs and any photography are yours forever. The website (Tier 2 & 3) remains accessible while the monthly fee is active. If we part ways, the brand and content are exported and handed over.
