04 Usage

Basic Usage

A short list of behaviours that protect the brand. Restraint is the operating principle — every piece should feel quiet and considered.

Українською

Прості правила — що робити і чого уникати, щоб бренд завжди виглядав узгоджено й преміально.

✓ Do
  • Set the wordmark in antique gold on mallard green wherever possible.
  • Pair Cormorant Garamond with Inter — these two only.
  • Use generous letter-spacing on all-caps Inter (0.32em–0.5em).
  • Keep ornaments (◆) small, sparse, and in gold.
  • Lead with mallard green as the dominant surface, gold as accent.
  • Treat italic Cormorant as the descriptive voice.
  • Maintain the cap-height clear space rule on all applications.
✕ Don't
  • Use bright, saturated, or metallic shiny gold — only the muted tone.
  • Place the wordmark on busy or photographic backgrounds.
  • Mix in a third typeface, including system or generic sans-serifs.
  • Stack the sub-line above the wordmark or break the lockup.
  • Apply drop shadows, outlines, or gradients to the wordmark.
  • Use red, blue, or pure white as decorative colour.
  • Set body copy in Cormorant under 18px — use Inter instead.
